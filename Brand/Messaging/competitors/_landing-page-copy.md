## Cross-web patterns (for the workshop)

1. **"Take control" is confirmed as the category's master verb on web too** — Aura (product page), DeleteMe ("Take Control of Your Online Privacy"), Kasper ("Ta kontroll över ditt digitala fotavtryck"), Google ("Take control of results about you" per app teardown). Incogni's "take *back*" remains the sharpest variant.
2. **Two explanation schools confirmed:** consequence-first (Cloaked's "destroying the inventory", PrivacyBee's threat blocks) vs mechanism-first (Optery's opt-out machinery, Incogni's 5-step pipeline). Nobody does both in one breath — same finding as the screenshot synthesis.
3. **The patent race is a web-only phenomenon:** Privacy Hawk prints four US patent numbers; Optery claims two. No app screenshot in the set shows patent numbers.
4. **Named-competitor comparison tables appear on web** (Aura vs Incogni vs Cloaked; Privacy Hawk's "How We Compare") but in no app screenshot — the web is where the category fights in public.
5. **DIY transparency is a web-native funnel:** Optery's self-service opt-out pages, PrivacyBee's "Excruciating Details," DeleteMe's opt-out guides hub — all web-only, all trust-building.
6. **Fear-stat inflation is trackable:** Aura's homepage says "$21 billion was lost in 2025" (FBI); the app teardown recorded "$12.5 billion." Same attribution, bigger number — worth verifying the current FBI figure before Vanyshr quotes any version.
7. **Enterprise splits:** Serus (Protection/Intelligence), Incogni (Ironwall), DeleteMe (business solutions), Guardio (Business tier), Self (enterprise branding/AI-personality section) — every scaled competitor segments consumer vs enterprise voice. Directly relevant to Vanyshr vs the Intelbase "enterprise version" framing.
8. **Serus is the only competitor that sells *investigation* as a consumer product** ("One search before you pay, sign, meet, or trust") — the intelligence page's "Some get to check. Everyone else guesses." is the strongest class-resentment hook in the set and the closest tonal cousin to what a Self-modeled Vanyshr could sound like.
